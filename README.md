## Why This Matters

Business Analytics helps organizations use data to understand performance, identify opportunities, solve problems, and make better decisions.

Business Strategy provides direction by defining where a business wants to compete, how it creates value, and how it can achieve its objectives.

## Learning Approach

Business Analytics and Strategy will be covered gradually, starting with business fundamentals and progressing toward analytics, metrics, financial concepts, market analysis, competitive strategy, decision-making, and data-driven business strategy.
